# Brew Calendar Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

Brew Calendar is a personal-use Windows desktop application designed to manage homebrewing schedules and synchronize events with Google Calendar.

## Information Accessed

Brew Calendar accesses locally stored BeerSmith brewing data, including recipe names, brewing dates, and scheduled brewing activities.

With user authorization, Brew Calendar accesses Google Calendar through the Google Calendar API to create, update, and delete brewing-related events.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to display and manage brewing schedules and synchronize brewing events with Google Calendar.

Brew Calendar does not sell personal information or use Google Calendar data for advertising.

## Data Storage and Sharing

Brewing information is stored locally in BeerSmith and, when synchronization is enabled, in the user's Google Calendar.

Google authorization credentials are stored locally on the user's computer.

Brew Calendar does not transmit user information to a developer-operated server or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

## User Control

Users can disconnect Google Calendar synchronization and revoke application access through their Google Account security settings.

Users can delete synchronized brewing events from Google Calendar.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email listed on the application's Google authorization screen.
